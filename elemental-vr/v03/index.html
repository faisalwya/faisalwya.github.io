This screen is a self-playing preview. Open this link in the Quest browser to play it yourself.

Take
Reach your open hand toward a fire, palm facing it. The flame flows to you and stays in your palm.
Throw
Push your palm forward. It is also how you let a flame go.
Charge
Make a fist, then open it toward a target.
Jet
Pinch index finger and thumb.
Controllers
Point at a fire with your arm out to pull it. Grip charges, trigger pours a jet, swing to throw. B or Y hides the wrist labels.
